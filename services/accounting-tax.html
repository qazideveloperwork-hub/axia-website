Accounting & Tax Services

Our trusted partners, comprising of licensed accounting, tax and audit professionals will support our clients in meeting Malaysia’s statutory financial and tax requirements.

Our partners will facilitate a full spectrum of services, including:

•
Statutory accounting and bookkeeping

•
Corporate tax registration and filing

•
Preparation of audited financial statements

•
Transfer pricing documentation and advisory

•
Payroll processing and employer tax submissions (PCB)

•
SST (Sales & Service Tax) compliance and reporting

This integrated support ensures our clients remain financially compliant while maintaining focus on their core business operations in Malaysia.
